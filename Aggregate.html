How to calculate your results

Add your O'Level points, your JAMB score divided by 8, and your Post-UTME score to obtain your aggregate score.

  1. O'Level Grade Points

    • A1 = 4.0 points
    • B2 = 3.6 points
    • B3 = 3.2 points
    • C4 = 2.8 points
    • C5 = 2.4 points
    • C6 = 2.0 points

    Add the points for your five relevant O'Level subjects.

  2. JAMB Score

    Add your Post-UTME score to the O'Level and JAMB points.
    Formula:
    Aggregate Score = O'Level Points + (JAMB Score ÷ 8) + Post-UTME Score
    Example:
    If your JAMB score is 320:
    320 ÷ 8 = 40

    Then add 40 to your O'Level points and Post-UTME score to get your aggregate score.
