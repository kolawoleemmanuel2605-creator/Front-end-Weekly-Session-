About UNILAG

The University of Lagos (UNILAG) is a prestigious public university located in Akoka, Lagos State, Nigeria. Established in 1962, the university is dedicated to providing quality education, research, innovation, and community development. UNILAG offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programmes across different fields, including science, technology, engineering, medicine, law, and the arts. The university is known for its diverse academic community and its contribution to the development of skilled professionals in Nigeria and beyond.

UNILAG offers a diverse range of academic programmes designed to develop students' knowledge, practical skills, and critical thinking. Through quality teaching, research, and innovation, the university prepares students to excel in their chosen fields and contribute meaningfully to society.
